# PRD: IndiNiger BizHub — Global Business Marketplace

## Executive Summary
IndiNiger BizHub is a premium, colourful, modern, fully responsive international business marketplace connecting multi-sector industries, entrepreneurs, manufacturers, suppliers, buyers, investors, and service providers worldwide.
- **Brand**: IndiNiger BizHub
- **Tagline**: "One Roof. Every Business. Global Opportunities."
- **Founder / Contact**: Kumud Gameti
- **Vision Scope**: 62 business categories, 57 countries (stated client vision, clearly documented and editable).

## 7 Core Pages Architecture
1. **Home (`/`)**:
   - Section A: Slim Announcement Bar
   - Section B: Sticky Navigation with Logo, Links, Industries Dropdown, Mobile Drawer, "Join Network" CTA
   - Section C: High-impact Split Hero with International Trade collage, Globe/Trade routes motif, CTAs
   - Section D: 4 Vision Highlight Cards (62 Categories, 57 Countries, Partnerships, Exhibitions)
   - Section E: Explore Business Categories Grid (17 detailed starter categories + extensible to 62)
   - Section F: Global Business Network with Interactive Country Selector & Trade Messaging
   - Section G: BUY, SELL, TRADE, CONNECT & GROW (5 interactive feature modules with working demo interaction)
   - Section H: Industries We Serve (High-quality visual cards)
   - Section I: Exhibitions & Trade Shows showcase + quick enquiry modal
   - Section J: How It Works (4-step clean horizontal/vertical process)
   - Section K: About IndiNiger BizHub (Vision, Kumud Gameti profile)
   - Section L: Partnership CTA banner
   - Section M: Full Lead & Enquiry Capture Form
   - Section N: Rich Global Corporate Footer

2. **Industries Directory (`/industries`)**:
   - Search by industry name / keywords
   - Filter by Sector (Industrial, Consumer & Lifestyle, Technology & Services, Global Trade)
   - Category cards with key statistics, description, sub-sectors, and direct link to directory

3. **Business Directory (`/directory`)**:
   - Filter by Industry & Country
   - Realtime keyword search across business names and offerings
   - Business cards with verified badges, products/services list, "View Details" drawer/modal, and "Send Enquiry" modal
   - Sample listings for real-world simulation

4. **Global Network (`/global-network`)**:
   - 57 Countries vision database organized by Continental Regions (Asia-Pacific, Middle East & North Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa, Europe, Americas)
   - Interactive country selector & market profile inspection (Trade focal areas, opportunities)
   - International collaboration enquiry trigger

5. **Exhibitions & Events (`/events`)**:
   - Upcoming International Trade Expos, B2B Summits, Manufacturing Conclaves
   - Filter by upcoming / virtual / in-person
   - Detailed event modal & direct Booth/Delegate Enquiry Form

6. **About Us (`/about`)**:
   - Founding vision, Kumud Gameti founder introduction
   - Core pillars: Trust, Scale, Multi-sector synergy
   - 62 Categories & 57 Countries roadmap milestones

7. **Contact Us (`/contact`)**:
   - Direct business enquiry form with validation
   - Demo storage inspection drawer (allows client to see their submitted test inquiries in real-time)
   - Office & International Trade hub placeholders
